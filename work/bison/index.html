The Brief

Roasted in the heart of the Three Valleys

Bison roast for the people who ride, ski and climb around Bozel and Courchevel. The identity had to look as sure of itself on a kraft bag as it does on a race poster.

We drew a bespoke typeface, set a tight palette of navy, cream and one loud orange, and wrote guidelines the team can pick up without us.
