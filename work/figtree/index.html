The Brief

Revenue management for build-to-rent

Figtree recommends a rent for every home in a build-to-rent portfolio, using machine learning and rules the operator sets. Its founders spent more than ten years running these buildings before they wrote a line of code.

They had a name and a product, but no brand. They needed one that asset managers and investors would trust with their pricing, and that didn't look like every other grey property dashboard. We started from scratch: logo, type, colour, art direction and the website.

Logo & Colour

Type

Gilroy Bold is kept for the wordmark. Everything else is set in Circular Light, large and loosely spaced, so the copy reads calm next to the bright colour.

Art Direction

Fine lines drawn over soft gradients, so the orange has texture without needing photography. The icon turns into a spark wherever Figtree's assistant is working.

Routes We Explored

We tried electric blues, then golds and greens with a family of sub-brands: Rent, Build, Tech, Home. The leaf survived every round. The colour settled on one warm orange, because the product is about homes.

Warm enough for a home. Sharp enough for a spreadsheet.
