The story

Built by operators

Figtree’s founders didn’t set out to start a software company. They spent more than ten years running build-to-rent buildings, and pricing every home in a spreadsheet.

That is still how most of the industry does it. Rents are set from spreadsheets and gut feel, and money is left on the table when demand moves. Figtree recommends a rent for every home, using machine learning inside rules the operator sets.

Most of its customers meet it first through its website. So we built the brand with the site in mind, then built the site: credible enough for an investment committee, warm enough to feel like it’s about homes.

TheWebsite

Website

A site that moves like the product

Three pages: Home, How it works and Built by Operators. As you scroll, the product rises out of the orange, the benefits stack like cards and a glowing light walks you through how the pricing works.

Built on Squarespace with custom code for the motion, so Figtree’s team can change the words themselves.

TheProduct

Illustration

Real dashboards are dense and change all the time, so we drew the product instead: simple cards, charts and maps in the brand colours, each animated to show what a feature does. They explain the platform faster than a screenshot and don’t go out of date.

TheLeaf

Logo

The wordmark is built from Gilroy Bold, heavy and plain, with one change: the dot of the i is a leaf. It’s the only flourish in the logo, so it carries the whole idea of steady growth.

On its own the leaf becomes the icon, cut out of a rounded square. Simple enough to read as a favicon, bold enough to sign off a slide.

Four colourways cover almost every use. The leaf stays orange wherever it can.

WarmOrange

Colour

Property technology defaults to blue. We went the other way, with an orange that feels like warm light rather than a warning sign. Peach gives it room to breathe, amber picks out highlights and a near-black charcoal carries the text.

It took a few rounds. Electric blues, then golds and greens, then a family of sub-brands. Each round we cut back, until one colour family was left.

Early rounds. The leaf survived every one.

SoftLight

Type and art direction

With the colour doing the shouting, the type stays calm: Circular Std Light for every headline and paragraph, Gilroy Bold only in the wordmark.

No photos of buildings. Soft orange gradients with fine contour lines drawn over them, part map of rising ground, part rising chart. The glowing icon sits on top, and turns into a spark wherever Figtree’s assistant is working.

OnPaper

Stationery

Business cards, a letterhead and a laptop sticker. The sticker carries the team’s six principles on the back, so the brand’s values travel with the people who wrote them.
